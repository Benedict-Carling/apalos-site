UK Biofoundries Network

One independent digital home for the network of automated biology facilities across the United Kingdom.

The challenge

Five biofoundries needed to be presented as one collaborative UK network while preserving the identity, expertise, facilities and leadership of each individual organisation.

Our approach

Apalos created a shared information structure, facility directory and visual system, then worked through detailed stakeholder feedback to keep complex research capabilities clear and approachable.

The outcome

The network now has a public, shareable website for collaboration and funding conversations, with dedicated pages for Edinburgh, Earlham, London, Manchester and the National Physical Laboratory.
