A bilingual collaboration platform connecting leading UK and Korean researchers in engineering biology. Built with Next.js and full Korean/English internationalisation, the site showcases the partnership's research, webinars, news, and project partners.

Features

Bilingual Platform
Full Korean and English internationalisation, allowing seamless switching between languages.
Webinar Series
Monthly webinars showcasing cutting-edge UK and Korean research in engineering and synthetic biology.
News & Activities
Latest updates on collaborative research, symposiums, and partnership milestones.
Project Partners
Profiles of partner institutions including Imperial College London, KAIST, University of Edinburgh, Manchester, and KRIBB.
Next.js & Modern Stack
Built with Next.js, delivering fast page loads, SEO optimisation, and a responsive design.
Research Focus
Centred on biofoundry developments, enabling co-development and sharing of synthetic biology protocols and workflows.
