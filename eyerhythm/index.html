EyeRhythm

Intelligent eye wellness monitoring

Available Now
Visit EyeRhythm

EyeRhythm monitors your natural blink patterns and suggests breaks only when your eyes actually need them. Unlike traditional timer-based reminders, EyeRhythm uses real-time analysis to protect your eye health based on your actual physiology, not arbitrary schedules.

Features

Blink Pattern Analysis
Real-time monitoring that triggers alerts based on your physiology, not arbitrary timers.
Wellness Scores
Track your eye wellness like a fitness tracker monitors steps, with 0-100 scores and trend analysis.
Complete Privacy
All processing happens on your device. No video data ever leaves your computer.
Works Offline
Real-time detection with no dependency on cloud services. Works perfectly without internet.
Native macOS App
Auto-start on power on with seamless menu bar integration for effortless monitoring.
Cross-Platform
Available for macOS, Windows, and Linux, plus a web version requiring no downloads.
