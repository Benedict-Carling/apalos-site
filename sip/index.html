Sip: Hydration

Smart hydration tracking for iOS

Available Now
Download on the App Store

Stay hydrated with Sip, the smart hydration tracker that learns your habits. Track your water intake with one tap, get personalized hydration goals based on your weight and activity level, and sync seamlessly with Apple Health.

Features

One-Tap Logging
Log drinks instantly with customizable quick-add buttons and haptic feedback.
Smart Goals
Personalized hydration targets based on your weight and activity level.
Apple Health Sync
Automatically sync your water and caffeine intake with Apple Health.
Smart Reminders
Gentle notifications only when you're behind on your hydration goal.
Beverage Tracking
Track water, coffee, tea, and more with accurate hydration coefficients.
Widgets
Beautiful home screen and lock screen widgets to track progress at a glance.
