Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Summary: Paige stores your lesson progress locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

Introduction

Paige: Learn Braille ("Paige", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Data We Collect

Paige is designed with privacy as a core principle. We collect and store the following data locally on your device only:

  • Lesson progress: Your current chapter, completed lessons, and level scores
  • Game data: High scores, daily streaks, and game statistics
  • User preferences: Theme settings, text size, notification preferences, and accessibility configurations

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. Your data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any external servers
  • Is not shared with any third parties
  • Is protected by your device's security features

No Account Required

Paige does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Analytics and Tracking

Paige does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not track your usage patterns or collect any anonymous usage data.

In-App Purchases

Paige offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all content. This transaction is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your Paige data at any time by:

  • Resetting your progress within the app settings
  • Uninstalling the Paige app from your device (this removes all locally stored data)

Children's Privacy

Paige is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
