Paige: Learn Braille

Your friendly guide to Unified English Braille

Available Now
Download on the App Store

Learn to read braille with Paige. Whether you're a teacher, parent, or simply curious, master braille reading through bite-sized lessons, fun games, and a built-in translator. 13 chapters take you from the alphabet through Grade 2 contractions and shortforms.

Features

Structured Curriculum
13 chapters from the alphabet through Grade 2 contractions, building real reading fluency.
Mini-Games
Daily braille Wordle, Dot Builder against the clock, and Streak Run rapid-fire identification.
Braille Translator
Type any text and see it in braille instantly, supporting both Grade 1 and Grade 2 UEB.
VoiceOver Support
Full VoiceOver accessibility throughout with haptic and audio feedback, all configurable.
Braille Keyboard
Connect an external braille keyboard for practice with optional musical dot feedback.
No Subscription
One-time purchase unlocks everything. No ads, no tracking. Light, dark, and system themes.
