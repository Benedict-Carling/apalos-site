2. Description of Service

Paige is a braille learning application designed to teach Unified English Braille (UEB) reading through structured lessons, games, and reference tools. The App provides:

  • 13 chapters of structured braille reading lessons
  • Mini-games for practising braille recognition
  • A braille translator supporting Grade 1 and Grade 2 UEB
  • A reference library of braille characters, contractions, and shortforms
  • External braille keyboard support
